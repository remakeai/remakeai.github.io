## Design Guidelines

### Tone

This page is **visionary and credible**, not salesy:

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "We're building the platform layer for robotics" | "Buy now! Limited time!" |
| "Here's our roadmap" | "Sign up immediately!" |
| Professional, confident | Hypey, startup-bro |
| Acknowledge what's not built yet | Overpromise |
| Platform company gravitas | Scrappy startup energy |

Think: How would Google describe Android to Samsung in 2008.

### Visual Style

- **Clean, professional** — Think enterprise SaaS or platform company
- **Generous whitespace** — Let the vision breathe
- **Diagrams** — The ecosystem stack diagram is important
- **Timeline visualization** — Show roadmap clearly
- **Robot imagery** — Show variety (vacuum, quadruped, drone, humanoid)
- **No playful/kids imagery** — That's for consumer pages; this is B2B

### Colors

- **Primary:** Deep blue or dark teal (trust, technology, platform)
- **Secondary:** Subtle grays
- **Accent:** Minimal — credibility over excitement
- **Avoid:** Bright playful colors from consumer pages

### Typography

- Clean sans-serif
- Larger, more generous than typical landing pages
- Hierarchy: Clear H1 > H2 > H3
- Pull quotes for key vision statements

### Layout

- Full-width sections
- Centered content with max-width
- Diagram section should be visually prominent
- Robot types section could use cards or columns
