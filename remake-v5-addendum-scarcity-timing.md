## Tone Guidance

Keep scarcity messaging **factual, not pushy**:

✅ Do:
- "First 2,000 units"
- "Reservations filled in order received"
- "Targeting holiday 2026 delivery"
- "Our goal: under your tree by Christmas"

❌ Don't:
- "ONLY 2,000 LEFT!"
- "ORDER NOW BEFORE IT'S TOO LATE!"
- "GUARANTEED Christmas delivery!" (you can't guarantee this)
- Countdown timers or fake urgency

The $1 price already reduces friction. Scarcity should feel like honest information, not pressure.
