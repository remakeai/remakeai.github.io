What to Expect

Works great on existing vacuums:

  • Hide-and-seek — Robot hides, kids seek (or vice versa)
  • Patrol mode — Security rounds, room checks
  • Follow-me — Robot follows you around
  • Custom routines — Advanced scheduling and automation
  • Home Assistant integration — Trigger any smart home action

Limited on existing hardware:

  • Ball games — Your vacuum isn't fast enough to hit a ball
  • High-speed chase — Limited by vacuum's max speed (~0.3 m/s)
  • Some games designed for faster movement
Why? Your vacuum was designed to clean, not to play. It prioritizes thorough coverage over speed.

Want all games at full speed? Two options:
• Developer hardware kits below ($150) — Full speed, no limits
• Consumer hardware at remake.ai/reserve ($199+) — Ready to use
